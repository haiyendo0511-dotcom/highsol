## Extracted direction

- Use architectural lines and quiet pool horizons rather than tropical decoration.
- Frame parasols as part of the resort design, not as isolated catalogue objects.
- Prefer cool daylight, graphite hardware, white canopies, mineral stone, and controlled greenery.
- Pair broad resort scenes with one precise engineering macro.
- Leave generous negative space and avoid crowds, stock lifestyle poses, and branded references.
