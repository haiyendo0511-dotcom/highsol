## Recommended product taxonomy

### 1. Center-pole parasols

The mast sits at or near the center of the canopy. This is the foundational hospitality format for dining tables, pool decks, beach clubs, and repeated terrace layouts because it creates a compact, visually ordered footprint.

Meaningful subcategories for the Parasols page:

- **Classic contract / market:** square, octagonal, hexagonal, or rectangular canopies with a conventional profile.
- **Marine / flexible-rib:** wind-oriented constructions with flexible or reinforced ribs for exposed resort and coastal settings.
- **Decorative canopy profile:** pagoda, valanced, manta, crescent, or other silhouette-led variants where visual identity matters as much as coverage.
- **Compact table parasol:** smaller spans for restaurant tables, balconies, and tighter layouts.

### 2. Cantilever / offset parasols

The canopy is suspended from a mast outside the shaded footprint. This keeps the center clear for dining, lounges, pool edges, and circulation. Premium models commonly add rotation, tilt, crank or assisted lift, and a telescoping closing motion that clears furniture.

Meaningful subcategories for the Parasols page:

- **Single-canopy cantilever:** one square, octagonal, or rectangular canopy on a side mast.
- **Rotating / tilting cantilever:** adjustable shade direction for all-day sun control.
- **Telescoping-mast cantilever:** raises during closure so tables and furniture can remain in place.
- **Multi-canopy shade pod:** two or four canopies supported by one mast for large dining or lounge zones; source dedicated licensed imagery before featuring this subcategory visually.

### 3. Large-format / telescopic architectural parasols

These are engineered structures for large hospitality terraces, event venues, public spaces, and landmark projects. A telescopic mechanism raises the canopy as it closes, helping clear tables and furniture. At the architectural end of the range, tensile membranes, integrated drainage, lighting, heating, motors, and wind controls become defining features.

Meaningful subcategories for the Parasols page:

- **Large contract telescopic:** approximately 4-7 m class hospitality umbrellas designed to close over furniture.
- **Giant architectural / XXL:** landmark-scale structures that require project engineering, foundations, wind assessment, and installation planning.
- **Tulip / reverse-conical membrane:** upward-facing architectural form with an expressive silhouette and potential integrated drainage.
- **Modular multi-unit array:** repeated large umbrellas linked by layout, gutters, or controls to cover broad terraces and public spaces.
