# Highsol launch replacement checklist

The current site is a complete visual concept. Do not publish it as a factual company website until these items are replaced or verified.

- Replace all four `*-concept.png` website images with real Highsol product and project photography.
- Add real factory photography. Do not use generated factory imagery.
- Confirm the factory city and address.
- Replace `inquiry@highsol.example` with the business email.
- Add the WhatsApp number in international format.
- Create a Formspree form and set `NEXT_PUBLIC_FORMSPREE_ID`.
- Confirm product collection names.
- Verify materials, wind ratings, warranties, service claims, certifications, capacity, lead times, and export markets before adding them.
- Add company registration, privacy policy, and domain details.
- Add real project names, clients, testimonials, or logos only with permission.
